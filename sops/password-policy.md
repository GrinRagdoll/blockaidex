# Standard Operating Procedure: Passwords and Account Recovery

**Audience:** All employees and administrators  
**Applies to:** Organization-managed accounts and authentication services

## Password requirements

- Choose a password at least **12 characters** long. A longer passphrase is recommended.
- Do not reuse a company password on personal or external services.
- Do not use shared, predictable, or easily guessed passwords, including names, common phrases, or sequential characters.
- Store passwords only in an organization-approved password manager. Never send passwords or MFA codes by email or chat.
- Change a password immediately if it may have been exposed, and report the suspected exposure to IT.

Passwords expire every **90 days**. Change the password through the official company sign-in or account-management page before expiry. Do not follow password-reset links from unsolicited messages; navigate to the known company portal directly.

## MFA requirement

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for company accounts. Enroll and maintain an IT-approved authenticator or security key as directed by the onboarding process. Approve only sign-in requests you initiated. Deny unexpected prompts and report repeated prompts to IT.

## Self-service password reset

1. Open the official company sign-in page and select **Forgot password** or **Reset password**.
2. Verify your identity using the registered recovery method and complete the MFA challenge.
3. Set a new password that meets the 12-character minimum and is not a recent or reused password.
4. Sign in again with the new password and complete MFA. Update it only in approved password-manager entries.

Self-service reset is available only when your account is active and you can complete the registered identity checks. Do not use another employee's recovery method or ask a colleague to reset your account.

## When self-service recovery fails

Contact the IT service desk using the published company support channel if you cannot access your registered MFA method, your account is locked, or reset verification fails. IT will verify your identity before restoring access. If IT issues a temporary password, use it only once, keep it private, and change it at the next sign-in; do not store or transmit it in plain text.

## Administrator handling

Administrators must use their assigned admin accounts, keep MFA enabled, and never request or collect a user's current password. Reset requests must be identity-verified and recorded through the approved support workflow.
